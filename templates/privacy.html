Privacy Policy

Welcome to MangaNoka. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and your options regarding this data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) unless you explicitly choose to create an account or contact us. For guest readers, all operations are completely anonymous.

Local Storage & Cookies

MangaNoka uses local storage to save your guest reading history (recently read chapters) and cookie-less localStorage values for your selected UI theme (light or dark mode). This information is stored strictly on your local device and is never sent to our servers.

2. Server Logs & Scraper Usage

Like most websites, our FastAPI servers temporarily log standard connection details (IP address, browser type, timestamp) to prevent malicious activity, DDoS attacks, and abuse. This data is rotated and automatically deleted periodically.

3. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be published directly on this page. We recommend checking back periodically to stay informed.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to our team via our official channels.
